Many AI tools run in a cloud somewhere. For an Abu Dhabi hospital, where that somewhere is can decide whether the tool may be used at all. Two Department of Health (DoH) standards answer the question: the Data Access Standard (published January 2026) and the Data Sharing, Integration and Interoperability Standard (published December 2025, effective April 2026).

## What the Data Access Standard says

The standard says no storage, replication or transfer of healthcare data outside the UAE may occur without explicit DoH authorisation. Remote access from outside the UAE is permitted only in exceptional circumstances with prior written approval from DoH. Entities that use external or cloud providers must keep data residency within the UAE, with contractual and technical safeguards against unauthorised cross-border transfer or access. The standard takes effect six months from the date of publishing.

## What the sharing standard adds

The data sharing standard says that data labelled under the data classification standard must not be transferred, stored, shared, processed or disseminated outside the UAE unless DoH issues a valid and specific exemption.

## What this means when a vendor pitches you

“Our model runs on a global cloud” is not an answer. Ask for these in writing:

1. Where is our data stored, including logs and backups?
2. Where is it processed, including by any sub-processor?
3. Does anyone outside the UAE have remote access, for support or otherwise? On whose written approval?
4. Which contract clauses and technical measures stop unauthorised cross-border transfer?
5. If an exemption is needed, who requests it, and has DoH granted it?

Staff who use free consumer chatbots are a separate risk: a hospital policy should say which tools are approved and which data may never be typed into any other tool. That is the author’s recommendation, not a quotation from the standard.

## What we did not read

This summary rests on the DoH documents. We could not read the federal laws themselves, because the federal legislation portal refused automated requests, so we do not say what federal law adds. For Dubai facilities, ask DHA and your counsel which rules apply.

## Key takeaways

- Abu Dhabi: no storage, replication or transfer of healthcare data outside the UAE without explicit DoH authorisation.
- Remote access from abroad needs prior written DoH approval and is only for exceptional circumstances.
- Cloud contracts must keep data residency in the UAE with contractual and technical safeguards.
- This is general information, not legal advice.
