An AI tool can produce a week of hospital posts in an afternoon. Hospital AI marketing in Dubai still has to meet the Dubai Health Authority's (DHA) [Standards for Medical Advertisement Content on Social Media](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/042022/Standards%20for%20Medical%20Advertisement%20Content%20in%20Social%20Media2022433965.pdf) (issue 1.1, effective 3 October 2022). It applies to DHA-licensed facilities and healthcare professionals. Abu Dhabi has its own requirements, which this article does not cover.

Here, "medical AI" means the broad field, "hospital AI" means AI used in hospital work, and "AI marketing" means using AI for promotion. These are working labels. The standard does not mention AI by name.

## 1. Who approves the post?

Content naming the facility or its location should be approved by the Medical Director, whether it comes from professionals, influencers or administrative staff (5.3). The facility's official account must state the medical advertisement licence number (5.2). Put AI-drafted posts through the same approval step.

## 2. Is there documented consent?

The standard requires documented consent from any individual or patient used in social media advertising (6.10), and written consent for their pictures, images, videos and statements (9.1). Do not put patient photos into an image tool without it.

## 3. Check the wording

Absolute statements, exaggerated claims and alarming expressions must not be used (6.11). The examples include "the best", "the only", "safest", "100%", "has no side effects" and "pioneer". Outcomes must be substantiated and risks included (6.6). AI drafts are worth searching for exactly these words.

## 4. Declare benefits and protect privacy

A facility or professional receiving financial or material benefit for promotion should document and disclose the relationship (6.7). Patient health information must be protected on social media (see the responsibilities under Standard Four).

## Try it this week

- Give your team the banned-word examples as a search list.
- Add "consent on file" and "MD approval" fields to the content calendar.
- Agree that AI will not generate patients, doctors or testimonials (our advice).

## Key takeaways

- Dubai's social media advertising standard applies to AI-drafted posts like any other content from a DHA-licensed facility.
- Medical Director approval and documented patient consent come first.
- Superlatives and guarantees such as "the best" or "100%" must not be used.
- This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with your compliance team.
