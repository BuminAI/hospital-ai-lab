AI tools that read from or write to Abu Dhabi’s health information exchange, known as Malaffi, inherit its rules. The Department of Health (DoH) set them in its Policy on the Abu Dhabi Health Information Exchange (reference DOH/POL/STR/HIE/1.1/2020, version 1.1), effective 16 April 2020. Two parts matter most to administrators and nurse leaders: consent and breaches.

## Consent

DoH requires healthcare facilities to seek patient consent for sharing patient data through the exchange. Facilities are to build this into their standard consent forms and to tell patients that sharing relevant health information with the exchange is part of their treatment. Where consent is unavailable for any reason, for example for legacy data, DoH requires and authorises facilities to share and receive the data as set out in DoH’s instructions.

For an AI project this means: if your tool uses exchange data, check how consent was obtained and on what basis, and do not assume that agreement to share for treatment also covers other purposes. (Secondary use has its own standard; see our post on the Analytics and Reporting Standard.)

## Breaches: two clocks

The breach clause sets two deadlines:

- Affected patients must be notified without undue delay and in no event later than 60 days from discovery.
- The clause on the exchange’s duties says DoH must be notified as soon as reasonably practicable after a breach is determined, and in any event within 5 business days.

The same clause has the exchange develop internal breach procedures, investigate root causes and set preventive plans, and it holds the exchange responsible for unauthorised disclosure by its own staff.

## What to put in your own contract

When an AI vendor connects to the exchange or to your records that feed it:

1. Ask how quickly the vendor will tell you of any breach, so that you can meet the 60-day patient notice and any other clock that applies to you.
2. Ask who talks to patients and who talks to DoH.
3. Ask for the breach procedure in writing.

## Key takeaways

- Exchange consent is sought from patients; legacy data may be shared as DoH instructs.
- Breach clocks in the exchange policy: patients within 60 days of discovery; DoH within 5 business days of determining a breach occurred.
- Put the clocks in vendor contracts and on your incident card. This is general information, not legal advice; the federal laws were not read for this post.
