When software that counts as part of a medical device misbehaves, the hospital’s first question is “who do we tell, and by when?” In Abu Dhabi the answer sits in the Department of Health’s Standard on Medical Device Reporting (MDR), version 2, published in July 2026 and effective October 2026.

## The three clocks

- **Five days.** For deaths, serious injuries and malfunctions, and events that need remedial action to prevent an unreasonable risk of substantial harm to public health, the standard says report as soon as possible from the date of awareness and not later than five days. Reporting may start as an initial report if follow-up reports come within 15 days.
- **Fifteen days.** Manufacturers and others who place devices on the market must submit, within 15 days after receipt, required information on deaths, serious injuries and malfunctions that was not available when the initial report was filed.
- **Ninety days.** Other reports, such as use errors, product quality issues and therapeutic failures, are due within 90 days of receiving the related reports, when the reporting is in the form of complete reports.

## Who reports

Healthcare facilities and professionals, and manufacturers, marketing authorisation holders, importers, authorised agents, distributors and anyone who places a device on the market.

## Which channel

Facilities and professionals with access to the Malaffi platform must use it as the primary method. Those without access, or during downtime, use the online fillable PDF form.

## A practical card for the nurses’ station

1. Something harmful or dangerous happened with a device or its software: tell the person named on your incident card at once.
2. That person reports to DoH as soon as possible, and no later than five days from awareness.
3. Keep the vendor in the loop; they have their own duties.
4. Lower-severity issues such as use errors go into the 90-day route.

Connected-device cyber incidents have a separate, faster route; see our post on the DoH cybersecurity guideline.

## Key takeaways

- Serious device events: report as soon as possible and not later than five days; follow-ups within 15 days; other reports within 90 days.
- Use Malaffi as the primary route if you have access.
- Put the clocks on one incident card. This is general information, not legal advice.
