An AI tool produces risk scores, draft notes or transcripts. How long should the hospital keep them, and where? In Abu Dhabi, the Department of Health (DoH) has a Data Storage and Retention Standard. This post reads its headline rule in plain words. It is general information, not legal advice.

## What the standard says

The DoH Data Storage and Retention Standard (DoH/ST/DGGO/DSR/V1/2025) says the minimum retention period for health data is 25 years, as the standard says UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 mandates. Otherwise, data should be kept only as long as necessary, with periods defined and documented by DoH's Data and Digital Governance Office.

## Questions for AI tools

These are our suggestions, not requirements in the standard:

1. **Which AI outputs are part of the health record?** Ask your health information team and compliance officer.
2. **Does the vendor keep a copy, and for how long?** Get the answer in writing.
3. **Who owns the retention schedule for each tool?** Name a person.

## Key takeaways

- The DoH standard sets a minimum retention period of 25 years for health data.
- Decide which AI outputs count as health data and where copies sit.
- Confirm with your compliance team. This is general information, not legal advice.
