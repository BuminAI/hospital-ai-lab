Hospital AI marketing makes it easy to produce captions, images and patient-facing messages quickly. Singapore's healthcare advertising rules still apply to what you publish. Before posting, check these four points from the Ministry of Health's (MOH) [FAQs on the Healthcare Services (Advertisement) Regulations](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/7/1d84b66a-5625-49a2-8c0e-e06eea4ed9fd/FAQs%20on%20HCS%20(Advertisement)%20Regulations_1.1.pdf), updated November 2024.

In this article, "medical AI" means the broad field, "hospital AI" means AI used in hospital work, and "AI marketing" means using AI for promotion. These are working labels. The FAQs we read do not mention AI by name.

## 1. Who is publishing?

The FAQs state that only a licensee or a person acting on a licensee's authority may advertise licensable healthcare services, under section 31 of the Healthcare Services Act. If an agency or an automated tool posts for you, confirm who has authorised it and who checks the output.

## 2. Can every claim be substantiated?

Regulation 5 requires information to be factually accurate and capable of being substantiated. The FAQs explain that this means credible, authoritative evidence, which should be cited in the advertisement and given to MOH on request. Check each sentence an AI draft states as fact.

## 3. Praise and before-and-after images

According to the FAQs, laudatory statements are strictly prohibited even if accurate, and advertisements must not feature before-and-after or after-only treatment photographs or videos, even with disclaimers. Promises of results within a certain time are given as an example of soliciting.

## 4. Reviews and testimonials

Under Regulation 14, licensees may not reproduce testimonials or endorsements, including reposting a user's post. Testimonials that patients gave directly to the institution may be displayed, if organic and not induced. Paid reviews are not allowed.

An AI-written review is not a patient's own account, so keep it out. That is this article's advice.

Patient data used for outreach is also subject to personal data law. This article does not cover it; check with your data protection officer.

## Try it this week

- Read three recent posts for praise words and unprovable claims.
- Note the evidence source beside every factual claim.
- Agree that no AI-generated patient, doctor or review is published.
- Planning a new AI tool? Run the [adoption checklist](/sg/checklist/).

## Key takeaways

- Hospital AI marketing follows the same advertising rules as any other campaign.
- Only licensees or their authorised persons may advertise licensable healthcare services.
- Claims must be accurate and capable of substantiation; laudatory statements are prohibited.
- This is general information, not legal advice. Check the current regulations and ask MOH or counsel.
