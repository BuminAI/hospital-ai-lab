A hospital information system and an AI tool that sits on top of it are often bought together. Under India’s medical device rules they do not always stand in the same place. CDSCO’s Guidance Document on Medical Device Software gives practical examples of software that is not a medical device.

## The examples CDSCO gives

In section 5.2, the guidance lists illustrations of software that is not Medical Device Software. They include:

- hospital or clinical information systems that support patient data management and are intended only for patient admission, scheduling appointments or visits, insurance and billing, clinical communication such as voice or video calling, and storing and transferring patient information;
- general communication systems that carry both medical and non-medical information, such as email;
- laboratory information systems whose main intended use is managing and validating information coming from analysers; and
- software that only transfers, stores, archives, converts, formats or performs simple searches.

The guidance says the examples are illustrations.

## Why the intended use is the test

What makes software a medical device is its intended use as the manufacturer states it, not the label “HIS” or “AI”. A scheduling system that only schedules is on the list. If the same product adds a function that analyses patient data to support a diagnosis or a treatment decision, the question changes. This is why the guidance says the class rests on intended use.

## What to do with this

1. Write down, for each system in your hospital, what the vendor states it is intended for.
2. For an AI add-on to a system on the “not a medical device” list, ask whether the add-on has its own intended use statement and licence.
3. Keep the vendor’s answer with the contract.

## Key takeaways

- CDSCO lists admission, scheduling, billing and similar hospital information systems as examples of software that is not a medical device.
- The test is the manufacturer’s stated intended use, so a new analytic function can change the answer.
- Ask about each AI add-on separately from the system it plugs into.
