Hospital AI marketing can mean a drafted newsletter, a generated image or a chatbot on the website. Each touches rules that already apply or now apply. Check these four before publishing.

Here, "medical AI" means the broad field, "hospital AI" means AI used in hospital work, and "AI marketing" means using AI for promotion. These are working labels.

## 1. Health data needs more than a good idea

[GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) Article 9 prohibits processing data concerning health unless one of its listed exceptions applies, such as explicit consent. A mailing list built from who attended a clinic can reveal health information. Settle the legal basis before any list goes near an AI tool.

## 2. People can object to direct marketing

Under GDPR Article 21(2) and (3), a person may object at any time to processing for direct marketing, and the data must then no longer be used for it. The right has to be brought to their attention at the latest at the first communication (Article 21(4)).

## 3. Say when it is AI

Article 50 of the [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) has applied since 2 August 2026. Providers must design systems that interact with people so that they are informed it is AI, unless obvious. Those deploying AI that creates or alters image, audio or video constituting a deep fake must disclose that it was artificially generated or manipulated.

Providers of generative AI also have machine-readable marking duties; under [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj), systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2 December 2026 to comply.

## 4. Doctors' advertising duties

The Medical Council's [Guide to Professional Conduct and Ethics](https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/news-and-publications/publications/guide-to-professional-conduct-and-ethics-for-registered-medical-practitioners-2024.pdf) says advertising of a doctor's services should be responsible, accurate, ethical, legal and truthful (50.3.1), and doctors must not make claims for the effectiveness of treatments that cannot be substantiated (50.2.1). These duties are addressed to registered medical practitioners.

Other rules, including electronic marketing regulations and the advertising code, are outside this article.

## Try it this week

- List your campaigns with the legal basis and how people can opt out.
- Add an "AI-generated" label wherever an image or voice could pass as real.
- Check every treatment claim in AI drafts against a source.
- Planning a new AI tool? Use the [adoption checklist](/ie/checklist/).

## Key takeaways

- Hospital AI marketing that uses health data needs an Article 9 exception such as explicit consent.
- People may object to direct marketing at any time, and use must then stop.
- Deep-fake images, audio or video must be disclosed under the EU AI Act.
- This is general information, not legal advice. Confirm with your data protection officer.
