Articles about a new HIPAA Security Rule often read as if it were already law. As of this writing, it is not. For a hospital that is planning a security budget around an AI project, it helps to separate what is proposed from what is required today.

## What happened

The Federal Register lists a proposed rule titled HIPAA Security Rule To Strengthen the Cybersecurity of Electronic Protected Health Information, published January 6, 2025 at 90 FR 898. Its comment period closed March 7, 2025. When we searched the Federal Register on November 6, 2026, we found no final rule for it. A proposed rule can be finalized, changed or withdrawn, and we do not report predictions about timing.

## What applies today

The current Security Rule text in 45 CFR Part 164, Subpart C remains in force. Several of its requirements bear directly on any new AI system that touches electronic protected health information:

- **Risk analysis.** A covered entity or business associate must conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of the risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the electronic protected health information it holds, and implement security measures sufficient to reduce those risks to a reasonable and appropriate level (§ 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A) and (B)). Both are required, not addressable.
- **Activity review.** Procedures must regularly review records of information system activity, such as audit logs and access reports (§ 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(D)), and information systems that hold or use the data need mechanisms that record and examine activity (§ 164.312(b)).
- **Encryption.** The current text lists encryption and decryption as an addressable implementation specification under access control (§ 164.312(a)(2)(iv)). Addressable means the entity must assess and document its decision, not that it can ignore it.
- **Documentation.** Policies and required records must be kept in writing for 6 years from creation or the date last in effect, and reviewed when operations change (§ 164.316(b)).

## A practical approach

Do not wait for a final rule to do the work that is already required. When a new AI tool is added, update the risk analysis to include it, check that logging covers it, and write down your encryption decision. If a final rule arrives and asks for more, you will already have the inventory and the records to build on.

## Key takeaways

- The HIPAA Security Rule rewrite was proposed on January 6, 2025 (90 FR 898); comments closed March 7, 2025; we found no final rule on November 6, 2026.
- The current Security Rule text still applies, including the required risk analysis and risk management.
- Add each new AI system to the risk analysis, logging and documentation you already keep.
