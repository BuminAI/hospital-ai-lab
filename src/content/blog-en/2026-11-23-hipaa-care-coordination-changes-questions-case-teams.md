Nurse case managers and discharge planners spend a lot of time asking a simple question: may I share this information with the next person who helps this patient? A final HIPAA Privacy Rule published on November 19, 2026 touches exactly that area. Our earlier post covered the shorter deadline for records requests. This one looks at the care coordination part, using only the rule's summary sections. We have not read the detailed provisions.

## What the summary says about care coordination

The rule is a final rule from HHS, published at 91 FR 74848, and it amends 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164. Its summary of major provisions lists several changes aimed at care coordination and case management. It says the rule will clarify the scope of permitted uses and disclosures for individual-level care coordination, and clarify the ability of covered entities to disclose protected health information (PHI) to social services agencies and similar third parties to facilitate care coordination and case management for individuals. The summary also lists a new exception topic for "individual-level care coordination and case management" under the minimum necessary standard in its table of contents, but we did not read that section, so we cannot say what it requires.

## Dates and what we did not read

The rule is effective on January 19, 2027. Persons subject to it must comply by July 19, 2027. Business associate agreements get a longer transition period, which the rule places in 45 CFR 164.532(e). We read the summary, major provisions, and dates sections only. Anything about exact wording, conditions, or what counts as a permitted disclosure sits in the detailed sections, which we have not reviewed.

## Questions to ask this month

These are our suggestions, not requirements from the rule, and they are not legal advice.

1. **Where do our staff get answers today?** Ask case managers which disclosure questions they escalate, such as sharing with community or social service organizations.
2. **Who owns the interpretation?** Ask the privacy officer to read the detailed sections of the rule and say which current policies it may affect.
3. **Which agreements are involved?** Ask compliance to list business associate agreements that may need review during the longer transition period.
4. **What will staff be told, and when?** Plan training around the July 19, 2027 compliance date rather than around the effective date alone.

## Key takeaways

- HHS published a final HIPAA Privacy Rule on November 19, 2026. It is effective January 19, 2027, with compliance required by July 19, 2027.
- The summary says the rule clarifies disclosures for care coordination and case management, including to social services agencies.
- We read only the summary and dates, so ask your privacy officer to read the full rule. This is general information, not legal advice.
