When a patient asks for a copy of their records, someone in health information management (HIM) or at the front desk starts a clock. That clock is about to get shorter. On November 19, 2026, HHS published a final rule changing the HIPAA Privacy Rule. It is not an AI rule, but it affects the same teams that handle patient data requests, including requests that arrive through apps. Here is what the Federal Register document says, based on its summary and dates sections.

## The key dates

The rule is effective on January 19, 2027. Covered entities must comply by July 19, 2027. It amends 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 and builds on a proposal HHS published in 2021.

## What changes for access requests

According to the rule's summary of major provisions:

- **Shorter deadline.** The time to respond to an individual's request for access drops to no more than 15 calendar days after receipt, down from the current 30 days. An extension of up to 30 calendar days remains available.
- **Fee schedules online.** Covered entities must post estimated fee schedules on their websites. The rule also changes when electronic copies must be free; we have not reviewed those details.
- **Less identity-verification burden** for people exercising their access rights, and clearer rules on the form and format of responses.
- **New definitions** of "electronic health record" and "personal health application."

## A change at registration

The rule eliminates the requirement for a provider with a direct treatment relationship to obtain the patient's written acknowledgment of receiving the Notice of Privacy Practices. The notice itself still exists, and its content requirements change. We have not reviewed the detailed section on the new content.

## What to prepare now

These are our suggestions, not requirements from the rule, and they are not legal advice.

1. **Measure your current turnaround.** Ask HIM how many access requests last month took more than 15 days, and why.
2. **Map where requests arrive.** Mail, the portal, email, and apps may each land with a different person.
3. **Draft the website fee schedule** with your privacy officer and finance team.
4. **Plan the registration change** with front-desk and nursing intake staff before forms are updated.

## Key takeaways

- HHS published a final HIPAA Privacy Rule on November 19, 2026; it is effective January 19, 2027, with compliance by July 19, 2027.
- Access requests must be answered within 15 calendar days instead of 30, and estimated fee schedules must be posted online.
- Written acknowledgment of the Notice of Privacy Practices is no longer required. This is general information, not legal advice; confirm details with your compliance team.
